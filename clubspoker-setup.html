ClubsPoker Setup

How to Connect

ClubsPoker connects automatically — no tokens to copy. Just pick your browser, log in, and BettrBot captures the session for you.

1

Select the ClubsPoker Account

Open BettrBot and pick ClubsPoker from the account picker. You'll see a Browser dropdown in the connection panel.

2

Choose Your Browser

From the Browser dropdown, pick the browser you normally use — Chrome, Edge, or Brave.

3

Click Connect

Press Connect. A real browser window opens and loads ClubsPoker.com. Leave this window open.

4

Log In to ClubsPoker

If you're not already signed in, log into your ClubsPoker account in that window like you normally would.

5

Open Blackjack & Place One Bet

Open the Blackjack game — it looks like this:

Place one bet so a hand is dealt. BettrBot captures your session the moment the hand starts.

6

Start Playing

Switch back to BettrBot — the status will show Connected and your balance will appear. Set your bet and click Start.
