How to Connect

Follow these steps to connect BettrBot to Lonestar's Instant Blackjack. No DevTools and nothing to copy — BettrBot opens a secure browser window and detects your session automatically.

1

Open BettrBot and Select Lonestar

Launch BettrBot, then choose Lonestar from the list of sites.

2

Pick Your Browser and Click Connect

Under Connection, use the Browser dropdown to pick the browser you normally use for Lonestar — Chrome, Edge, or Brave — then click Connect.

BettrBot opens a secure browser window pointed at lonestarcasino.com. That window is all you need — no DevTools and nothing to copy.

3

Log Into Lonestar

In the window BettrBot just opened, log into your lonestarcasino.com account if you aren't already signed in.

4

Open Instant Blackjack and Play One Hand

Open the Instant Blackjack game, let the table finish loading, and place one hand.

That first hand lets BettrBot detect your live session — it happens automatically in the background, so there's nothing to copy or paste.

5

You're Connected

BettrBot captures your session on its own — your balance loads and the status turns to Connected. Set your bet size and press Start.

Keep the browser window BettrBot opened open while you play — it keeps your session alive. Closing it ends the session.
